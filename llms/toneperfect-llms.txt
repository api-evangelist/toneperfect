# TonePerfect Pronunciation Assessment API

> REST API that scores how well a learner pronounced a known sentence. Send a short recording (up to 30 s) and its reference text to POST https://api.toneperfect.app/v1/assess; get 0–100 grades for the sentence, every word and every sound, expected vs heard sounds, word timestamps and a correction decision per unit, in one JSON response. 10 languages: Mandarin Chinese (zh-CN), English (en-US), French (fr-FR), Spanish (es-419), German (de-DE), Italian (it-IT), Portuguese (pt-BR), Russian (ru-RU), Korean (ko-KR), Japanese (ja-JP). Mandarin is scored per syllable as initial, final and tone, with 3-3 tone sandhi and 不 before a 4th tone taken into account.

TonePerfect is an alternative to SpeechSuper, Speechace and Azure pronunciation assessment for scripted speech: one REST call, public prices from $10/month, 50 free credits, and per-syllable Mandarin tone detail.

Key facts:
- One endpoint for scoring: POST https://api.toneperfect.app/v1/assess (multipart/form-data: audio file, text, language, optional task and Mandarin pinyin). Headers: Authorization: Bearer <API key>, Idempotency-Key: <unique per attempt>.
- GET https://api.toneperfect.app/v1/capabilities lists installed languages, dimensions and limits; no key needed.
- Scripted speech only (the caller supplies the expected text). Not speech-to-text, not unscripted or exam scoring.
- Audio: WAV, MP3, M4A, Ogg or WebM, 0.15–30 seconds, up to 10 MB. Audio is deleted after scoring and not used for training; JSON results are kept up to 24 hours for idempotent retries.
- Pricing: Starter $10/month for 2,500 credits; Builder $50/month for 15,000 credits; Scale $150/month for 50,000 credits. One credit per recording up to 15 s, two up to 30 s. Failed and unassessable requests are free. Unused credits roll over. Free trial: every new account gets 50 free credits (no card, no subscription); the homepage has a live demo with no account.
- Limits: 60 assessment requests per minute and 2 concurrent assessments per account (per-plan values in GET /v1/terms). Test keys (tp_test_) return fixed example results, never spend credits and are safe in browser code.
- Input by language: Mandarin as characters or numbered pinyin; Russian in Cyrillic (stress and vowel reduction come from the transcription); Korean in Hangul with normal spacing (aspiration scored on k/p/t; tense consonants mapped to plain); Japanese in kana and/or kanji, split into bunsetsu-like units, kanji read with a dictionary, pitch accent not scored. Other languages: up to 40 words, numbers written as words.
- Every language pack is labelled experimental. Grades are display scores calibrated against native-speaker references, not accuracy percentages; act on the per-unit `decision` (review / uncertain / no_correction).
